Carry your coach in your pocket.

About the App

Coach Ann's Coaching Cards is a collection of 208 interactive coaching cards across four color-coded decks — designed for tennis players rated 3.0 and above and junior tournament competitors who want to compete with more confidence, sharper strategy, and cleaner technique.

Each card features a short coaching prompt on the front and a full explanation on the back. Tap to flip. Swipe to navigate. Shuffle the deck for a fresh order. Whether you're warming up before a match, resetting between sets, or reviewing after a tough loss, these cards meet you where you are.

The Four Decks

MINDSET DECK (Black) — 52 cards

Build grit and compete with fire. Direct, no-nonsense coaching cues that keep you locked in during pressure moments. Cards like "The point is over," "Compete, don't calculate," and "Nervous means ready." This is your coach-in-your-ear voice when the match gets tight.

MENTAL DECK (Purple) — 52 cards

Go deeper. This deck draws from breath work, philosophy, and the warrior mindset to build lasting inner strength. Cards like "Stay," "Breath is your first weapon," and "Consistency over power." For players ready to train the mind as seriously as the body.

TACTICAL DECK (Navy) — 52 cards

Sharpen your match IQ and outthink your opponent. Covers shot selection, pattern play, serving strategy, court positioning, and mid-match adjustments. Stop playing on autopilot and start constructing points with purpose.

TECHNICAL DECK (Green) — 52 cards

Refine your strokes with breathing-integrated technique cues. Covers groundstrokes, volleys, serves, returns, and footwork fundamentals — all connected to how your breath powers your movement. Because mechanics without rhythm is just swinging.
